# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 24 October 2026*

**Fonts for LinkedIn does not collect, store or transmit any data.**

- **No data collection.** The extension has no analytics, telemetry, crash
  reporting or tracking of any kind.
- **No network requests.** The extension never contacts any server, including
  the developer's.
- **No storage.** Nothing is saved: no settings, no history, no cookies, no
  local storage.
- **No permissions.** The extension requests no browser permissions. It only
  runs a script on pages of `https://www.linkedin.com/` to show the formatting
  toolbar.
- **Local processing only.** When you press a formatting button or shortcut,
  the selected text is transformed inside your browser, in the page you are
  editing, and nowhere else. The extension only looks at the text field you
  are editing (to locate your selection) and at the page's colours (to pick
  light or dark mode). That information never leaves the page and is
  forgotten immediately.

The extension is open source. You can verify all of the above in the code at
<https://github.com/napolux/linkedin-fonts>.
